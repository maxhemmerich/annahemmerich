About

Painting in acrylic, oil and collage, on canvas, panel and cut wood.

Each painting starts from a drawn structure — a net, a fence, a grid, a scaffold. Then the surface is covered over: flat blocks of paint, pasted paper, sawn pieces of wood, and outlines laid on last and left to wobble. The drawing underneath stays half visible.

The palette is mixed to stay saturated and mostly unmixed — chartreuse, vermilion, cobalt, ochre, hot pink. The structure holds the picture together while everything else is stacked on top of it.

Titles point somewhere — Tennis, Huckleberry bush, Garbage Collector — and the painting rarely follows all the way.
